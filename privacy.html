Privacy Policy

Crowder Community Forums (the "Forum") is a small hobby message board at darthdan.vpages.site/crowder-forums. Last updated: November 17, 2026.

What we collect

When you register, we store your email address, a forum username you choose, and your password (stored only as a salted hash by our sign-in provider, Supabase). If you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple", we receive your email address and, if the provider shares it, your name, which we use only to suggest a username. We never receive your Google or Apple password. We also store what you post: topics, replies, private messages, your optional avatar link, location and signature, and a last-seen time.

How we use it

Only to run the Forum: signing you in, showing your posts and profile, delivering private messages, and preventing spam and abuse. Your email address is never shown publicly. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not run ads or analytics.

Who processes it

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). If a human check is enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile may run in your browser to tell people from bots. Google or Apple handle the sign-in screen if you use those buttons.

Cookies and storage

Your browser stores a sign-in session token so you stay logged in. There are no advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time. To have your account and data deleted, send a private message to the administrator on the Forum.

Children

The Forum is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
